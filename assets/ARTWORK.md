## Raphael generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original vertical portrait illustration for the Fortune Teller's Monad card in AEON DESCENT, within David Cole's The Lion of God fantasy universe. Depict Archangel Raphael as a compassionate traveling healer, an androgynous youthful adult masculine angel with shoulder-length chestnut curls, composed face, broad ivory feathered wings, emerald and ivory flowing robes with restrained gold embroidered trim. Draw from the visual vocabulary of fifteenth-century Tobias-and-the-Angel paintings: the protective traveling angel, a slim pilgrim staff and small round healing vessel with a subtle fish clasp. Make an original composite, not a reproduction of a painting. Raphael kneels beside a weary injured adult traveler in a ruined garden, one wing sheltering the traveler while a warm light rests between Raphael's hands. The mood is gentle, steady, quietly powerful. Detailed comic-book line art, clear ink contours, painterly jewel-tone colors, etched gold highlights, luminous but readable face. Renaissance drapery with graphic-novel lighting. Portrait 2:3 composition, Raphael's head and wings in upper half, hands and healing light in center, the traveler low in the frame. Full-bleed illustration; NO text, NO typography, NO card border, NO watermark; all titles and frames will be drawn by the game UI.

## Jophiel generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story, reference-guided original character illustration. Asset type: vertical full-bleed illustration for Jophiel's Monad card in AEON DESCENT within David Cole's The Lion of God fantasy universe. Input image 1 is the user's Jophiel reference: preserve its feminine adult angel, luminous golden wings, flowing gold-white robes, radiant light above her raised hand, and glowing sacred-geometric sphere in her other hand. Input image 2 is a STYLE reference for the matching Raphael card: detailed comic-book ink contours, painterly jewel colors, readable faces, etched decorative gold, Renaissance drapery. Create Jophiel in that same graphic-novel style. She has long golden-blonde hair and a serene, artistically attentive expression, not a child. She stands amid a ruined atelier where carved fragments and painted panels regain their color in her light; she is the Archangel of Beauty and Artists. Golden feathered wings open upward, one arm raises a luminous point of light, other hand holds a golden geometric orb at shoulder level. Indigo/violet sky beyond an arched atelier; warm ivory, honey gold and luminous sunlight. Maintain the reference's uplifting cosmic femininity, with clear facial detail and coherent hands. Tall portrait 2:3 composition, full figure, head in upper-middle, both symbolic objects visible, safe margins around important elements. No text, no labels, no card frame, no watermark; the game UI provides names and borders.
