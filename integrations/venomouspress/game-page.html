Quest of the Bodhisattva

An esoteric fantasy RPG built on Open Sosaria, inspired by the spirit of classic Ultima. Let the Fortune Teller reveal your Monad, explore the Lantern Coast, and confront cyclopean horrors with gifts of light.

Visit Haven and Vesper, answer the Listener and Choir, and follow a seeded Lost Pilgrim adventure. Five Monads offer distinct powers and paths.

Free browser play · Desktop and mobile · Turn-based exploration · Original soundtrack

This game is in active development. The current chapter is playable; quests, features, and artwork may evolve as the adventure grows. This page receives game updates automatically.

Start your journey

Begin with the Fortune Teller, or choose your Monad directly. Seek Haven at (43,40). Arrow keys or WASD move; E interacts, F strikes, P uses your gift, and G escapes a road duel. On a phone, use the gamepad controls and map zoom.

Music begins after your first interaction. Use the game’s Music and Volume controls to adjust it. Saves stay in your browser; use Export and Import to carry a journey between devices or between embedded and separate-tab play.
